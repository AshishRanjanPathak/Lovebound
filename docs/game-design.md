# LOVEBOUND — Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Game Overview

* **Title**: LOVEBOUND
* **Genre**: 2D Side-Scrolling Action-Adventure
* **Target Audience**: Fans of anime fantasy adventures, tight platformers, and narrative-driven indies
* **Core Theme**: Unwavering devotion overcoming manipulation, deception, and greed

---

## 2. Narrative & Characters

### The Heroine — Astraea
* **Title**: Maiden of the Staff of Love
* **Appearance**: Anime magical heroine adorned in celestial robes with a crystalline staff.
* **Forms**:
  * **Normal State (Love Magic)**: Pink, radiant, empathetic magical bolts that weave light around enemies.
  * **Enraged State (Dominance Magic)**: When villains threaten to sever her bond with Elinore, righteous fury triggers a violet transformation. The staff becomes a conduit of dominance, dealing critical impact damage.

### The Companion — Princess Elinore
* **Role**: AI-controlled follow companion and support partner.
* **Behavior**: Hovers gracefully alongside Astraea, reacts to combat, and casts **Starlight Blessing** (a golden shield that protects Astraea from incoming projectiles).

### The Villains (Territory Bosses)
1. **HATA (Gremlin Woods)**: A manipulative trickster gremlin who poisons minds with deceit, trying to convince the maidens that love is a lie.
2. **BUNNY (Enchanted District)**: A deceptive enchantress who lures wanderers into unending glamorous masquerades to steal their memories.
3. **RISHI (Greedlands)**: A ferocious black boar titan wielding an enormous stone tablet inscribed with crushing debts and decrees.

---

## 3. World & Territory Progression

1. **Gremlin Woods (Canopy of Whispers)**:
   * Level 1+ introductory biome.
   * Bioluminescent moss, ancient twisted tree boughs, corrupted wisps.
   * Ancient Sanctuary Shrines serving as checkpoint auto-saves.

2. **Enchanted District (Gilded Labyrinth)**:
   * Level 5+ second territory.
   * Floating crystal pavilions, masquerade ballroom ruins, rose brambles.

3. **Greedlands (Crag of Golden Spires)**:
   * Level 10+ climactic territory.
   * Volcanic crags, gold-gilded ruins, colossal decree monuments.

---

## 4. Combat Mechanics

* **Staff Love Magic (K)**: Rapid-fire pink magical orbs tracking towards targets.
* **Basic Melee Strike (J)**: Quick physical staff arc for close combat and deflecting wisps.
* **Dodge Dash (Shift)**: Forward invulnerability dash through obstacles and enemy bolts.
* **Princess Blessing (E)**: 4-second protective starlight aura blocking enemy strikes.
* **Dominance Ultimate (Q)**: Activates at 100% Dominance Meter (charged by landing strikes). Astraea transforms into her violet dominance form with heightened speed, double damage, and screen rumble for 10 seconds.
